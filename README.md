# RazorGrow AI

### Turn Payment Data Into Revenue With an AI Growth Agent

RazorGrow AI is an AI-powered merchant growth platform that analyzes payment, customer, product, and transaction data to identify lost-revenue opportunities and recommend intelligent, merchant-approved actions.

> **Analyze → Understand → Recommend → Approve → Act → Measure**

---

## 🎯 Problem

Online merchants generate large amounts of payment and customer data, but often struggle to turn that data into actionable decisions.

They may have:

- Abandoned checkouts
- Failed payments
- Inactive customers
- Missed upsell opportunities
- Poorly performing products
- Customers with high purchase intent
- No clear understanding of why revenue is changing

Traditional dashboards can tell a merchant **what happened**, but they don't necessarily tell them:

> **Why did it happen, what should I do, and which action has the highest potential impact?**

---

## 💡 Solution

RazorGrow AI acts as an intelligent **AI Growth Agent** for merchants.

It analyzes merchant data, identifies revenue opportunities, prioritizes them, recommends actions, checks merchant-defined guardrails, and sends important actions through a human approval workflow.
